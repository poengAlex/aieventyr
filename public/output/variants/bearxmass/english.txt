Yes, the Bear did as the Fox had said and kept his tail in the hole for a long, long time until it was frozen solid; then he jerked it out sharply—and broke it off completely, and now he walks around stub-tailed to this very day.

The Fox tricks the Bear out of his Christmas feast.

Once upon a time, the Bear and the Fox bought a tub of butter together; it was meant for Christmas, so they hid it under a thick fir bush. Then they walked a bit further and lay down on a sunny slope to sleep. After a while, the Fox got up and shouted, "Yes!"—and dashed straight to the butter tub, where he ate a good third of it. When he returned and the Bear asked where he had been since he looked so greasy around the mouth, he said, "Would you believe I was invited to a christening?" "Oh really! What was the child's name?" asked the Bear. "Began-With," replied the Fox. With that, they lay down to sleep again. After a little while, the Fox sprang up once more and shouted, "Yes!" and off he went to the butter tub. This time he ate another good chunk. When he came back and the Bear again asked where he had been, he answered, "Oh, wouldn't you know I was invited to another christening?" "What was the child's name this time?" asked the Bear. "Half-Eaten," said the Fox. The Bear thought this was an unusual name, but he didn't ponder it for long before he yawned and fell asleep; no sooner had he been lying down for a short while than the same thing happened as the previous two times: the Fox jumped up again, shouted "Yes!" and raced over to the butter tub, from which he finished off the remainder. When he came back, he claimed to have been at a christening once more; and when the Bear wanted to know the child's name, he replied, "Scraped-the-Bottom!" With that, they lay down to sleep again for a while. Then they were supposed to go and check on the butter; but it was eaten up. So the Bear blamed the Fox, and the Fox blamed the Bear; each said the other had been to the butter tub while they themselves were asleep. "Well, well," said the Fox, "we'll soon find out which of us has stolen the butter. Now we'll lie over there on the sunny slope: whoever is the fattest at the end when we wake up, he has stolen it." Yes, the Bear was happy to try that test, and knowing he hadn’t even tasted the butter, he lay down quite confidently to sleep in the sun. Then the Fox snuck over to the butter tub for a bit of butter left in a crack and back to the Bear and smeared it on his end; after that, he lay down to sleep as if nothing had happened. When they both woke up, the sun had melted the butter, and it turned out that the Bear very clearly appeared to have eaten the butter.
