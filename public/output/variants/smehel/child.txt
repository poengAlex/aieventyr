First Story: The Sneaky Fox

Once upon a time, a fox was asked to look after a woman's animals: goats, sheep, and cows. But the fox was very sneaky! On the first day, he ate all the goats. The next day, he gobbled up all the sheep. On the third day, he couldn't resist and devoured all the cows too. 

When he returned home, the woman asked, "Where are my animals?" The fox cheekily said, "Their heads are in the river, and their tails are in the forest." 

The woman, busy making butter, felt she had to check on her animals. While she was away, the fox sneaked in and licked all the cream. When the woman came back and saw what he had done, she was so angry! She tried to whack him, but only a tiny bit of cream splashed onto the tip of his tail. That's why foxes have white tips on their tails.

Second Story: The Clever Smith

Once upon a time, Jesus and Saint Peter were wandering on earth and met a blacksmith who boasted that he was the best of all. The blacksmith had made a deal with the Devil to be the best for seven years, after which he'd belong to the Devil.

Jesus offered to show him how to shoe a horse a special way. He took the horse's legs off, hammered them perfectly, and put them back on. The blacksmith was amazed and impressed!

Later, Jesus did another amazing thing. He turned the blacksmith’s old mother into a young woman again! The blacksmith tried to do the same with his horse and a poor old lady, but they caught on fire instead. 

Seeing this, Jesus granted the blacksmith three wishes. The blacksmith wished anyone who climbed his pear tree would stay until he let them down, anyone who sat in his special chair would be stuck until he released them, and anyone who went into his pocket would stay until he allowed them out.

When the Devil came to collect the blacksmith, he tricked the Devil into getting stuck in his pear tree, then his chair, and finally locked him in his pocket. The Devil was so scared, he promised never to come back!

With laughs and lessons learned, these tales remind us to be clever and careful!
