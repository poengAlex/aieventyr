Once upon a time, he found a skull and took it with him. As he neared the King's palace, he stumbled upon an old groom's document, and after a bit more walking, he discovered a mackerel lying about, chattering, which he also picked up. 

When he arrived at the palace, he went to the King and stated that he had come to try to outwit the Princess. 
- "That's fine, my Son," said the King, "but do you have any great skills of wisdom that you plan to use to outwit her?"
- "I have some small things here," Askeladden replied, "that I think might do the trick." 

Once the King and the court were gathered, and the Princess had entered, Askeladden pulled out a bundle of sticks and said:
- "Yes, this is my first. I got it from a man who stole from the King's widowed estate, and later he got stuck in an earth hut." 
- "That's amusing," said the Princess; "but what was next?" 

Then he presented a broken bowl and said: 
- "This comes from the great argument where they fought over a pot of stew," Askeladden said. 
- "That is quite strange," said the Princess; "what is the third?" 

He then displayed the skull and stated:
- "This is from the great catastrophe in Death Valley; a gentleman went and found a deadly box filled with danger."
- "That's remarkable," the Princess said, becoming more amazed with each passing moment.
- "What is your fourth?"

Askeladden then drew out the groom's document and declared:
- "This is what someone used to save the bride, and once he was equipped, he returned home and escaped his in-laws." 

Now, the Princess could think of no more questions, feeling relieved and happy, wanting to say something, but not a word would pass her lips. 
- "Here you have my last item," he said, producing the mackerel: "This is from the great Mackerel's saga, which played; it flew and chattered and lay there."

When the onlookers heard how Askeladden had done it, they clapped their hands and said:
- "You have outwitted the Princess," they exclaimed; "you deserve her and half the kingdom." The Princess came to recognize her equal in the land and her crown upon her head, and Askeladden married her and became a king of the realm.
