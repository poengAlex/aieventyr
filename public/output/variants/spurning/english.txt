Once he entered the great hall of the king's palace, he opened the cabinet for the princesses. Recognizing their respective gold crowns, the youngest princess said, "I believe it is only fair that the one who truly saved us should receive his deserved reward, and it is not Sir Red, but the man who brought our gold crowns—he is the one who saved us." At that moment, the prince discarded his sailor's disguise and stood there more splendidly attired than all the others. The old king then ordered Sir Red to be executed. Finally, real joy filled the palace; everyone claimed their partner, and a wedding occurred, celebrated across twelve kingdoms.

Once upon a time, there was a king with a daughter so difficult and contrary in speech that none could silence her. He proclaimed that whoever managed to do so would win the princess's hand in marriage and half the kingdom. Three brothers, having heard this, decided to try their luck. The two eldest, thinking themselves the smartest, went first, but they couldn't outwit her and were punished. Then Askeladden set out on his journey. Along the way, he found a willow band, which he picked up. Continuing on, he discovered a bowl fragment and picked it up as well. Further along, he found a dead magpie, a crooked goat horn, and shortly after that, its match. As he crossed the fields near the palace, he came across a worn-out shoe sole. Gathering all these items, he went to the princess.

"Good day," he said. 

"Good day," she replied, shifting uncomfortably.

"Will I get my magpie fried?" he asked.

"I fear it might burst," said the princess.

"That’s no worry, I'll wrap this willow band around it," replied the boy, showing it to her.

"The fat will run off," she countered.

"I'll catch it with this," he said, presenting the bowl fragment.

"You speak so crookedly," said the princess.

"No, I'm not crooked, but this is," he replied, showing one of the goat horns.

"I’ve never seen the like!" exclaimed the princess.

"Here is its match," said the boy, revealing the other horn.

"I think you've come to silence me, haven't you?" she asked.

"No, I haven’t come to be worn out, but this is worn out," he replied, producing the shoe sole. 

Thus, the princess was silenced. "Now you are mine," said the boy. So he won her and half the kingdom as well.
