The image style inspired by the text can be described as follows:

**Style Description:**

- **Shading Style:** Soft shading. This style complements the fairy tale narrative by adding a dreamy, delicate quality to the illustrations. The transition and blending of colors are smooth, enhancing the whimsical feel of the story.
  
- **Linework:** Clean linework. This approach provides clarity and consistency to the characters and environments, maintaining a fairy tale quality without being overly simplistic or complex.
  
- **Color Palette:** Vibrant palette. Bright and vivid colors are used to convey the magical and fantastical elements of the story. This choice reflects the engaging and mystical ambiance depicted in the narrative.
  
- **Textures:** Smooth textures. Given the magical and storybook nature of the text, smooth textures help to further the dreamy and nostalgic feel, making objects and surfaces appear soft and inviting without any harshness.
  
- **Proportions:** Stylized proportions. The characters and elements are drawn with whimsical features to emphasize the fairy-tale aspect and to steer away from realistic depiction, adding to the charm and personality of the story's universe.

- **Lighting:** Atmospheric lighting. Soft, warm lighting enhances the magical atmosphere, suggesting an ethereal, otherworldly glow that guides the viewer through the story’s various settings.

Overall, this style captures the essence and whimsy of a traditional fairy tale, bringing to life the enchanting, folklore elements through a blend of vibrant colors, soft textures, and stylized character designs.
