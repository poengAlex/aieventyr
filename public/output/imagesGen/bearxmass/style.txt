The image style for this whimsical and playful folktale should reflect the story's lighthearted and mischievous nature. Here's a fitting image style description:

- **Shading**: Soft shading to give the illustrations a gentle and warm feel, emphasizing the playful and folkloric atmosphere of the story.

- **Linework**: Clean linework to depict the characters and their expressions clearly, ensuring that the storytelling is easily understood and visually engaging.

- **Color Palette**: A vibrant palette to capture the lively and mischievous tone of the tale. Bright, cheerful colors can bring out the playful nature of the characters, especially the cunning fox.

- **Textures**: Smooth textures to maintain a polished and inviting appearance, enhancing the fairy-tale quality of the illustration.

- **Proportions**: Stylized proportions to emphasize the whimsical and fantastical elements of the story. Slight exaggerations in the animals' expressions and actions can enhance humor and charm.

- **Lighting**: Atmospheric lighting to create a magical and storybook-like ambiance, focusing on creating a cozy and enchanted woodland setting where the tale unfolds.

This style choice will beautifully complement the narrative, drawing viewers into the enchanting world of the bear and the cunning fox, while conveying the moral and humor of the story effectively.
