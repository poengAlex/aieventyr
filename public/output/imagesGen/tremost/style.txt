The image style for this classic fairy tale scene should be characterized by:

1. **Gradient Shading**: The use of gradient shading will give the scene depth and a sense of magical realism, capturing the whimsical and ever-changing mood of the story.

3. **Vibrant Palette**: A vibrant color palette will bring life to the characters and setting, highlighting the contrast between the humble origins of the main character and the opulence of the royal court.

4. **Stylized Proportions**: The proportions should be stylized to emphasize the fairy tale nature of the story, with slightly exaggerated features for the characters, especially the "Mostre" with their distinctive appearances.

5. **Dramatic Lighting**: Dramatic lighting will create a contrast between the rich, warm scenes of the royal court and the darker, humble beginnings of the main character, enhancing the fairy tale atmosphere and highlighting the emotional highs and lows of the narrative.
