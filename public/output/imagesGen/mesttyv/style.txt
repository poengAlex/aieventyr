The image style for this narrative should feature a whimsical and fairy-tale-inspired aesthetic, reflecting the story's folklore roots. Here’s a detailed style description:

1. **Shading**: Employ gradient shading to give the characters and environment a slightly magical and soft look, enhancing the storybook feel without being overly realistic.

2. **Linework**: Utilize clean linework to provide clarity to the intricate details of the characters’ attire and expressions, ensuring the compositions are easy to read and vibrant.

3. **Color Palette**: Opt for a vibrant palette to capture the lively and dynamic nature of the story, emphasizing the fantastical elements and contrasting the different settings within the narrative, like the forest, the robbers' lair, and the countryside.

4. **Textures**: Integrate smooth textures to keep the scenes polished and visually appealing, creating a sense of continuity and coherence throughout the various story settings.

5. **Proportions**: Use stylized proportions to give the characters distinctive silhouettes that enhance their personalities—such as the youthful innocence of the protagonist or the sinister appearance of the robbers.

6. **Lighting**: Incorporate atmospheric lighting to underscore the narrative shifts, such as the eerie ambiance of the forest at night, the warm and inviting glow of the farmhouse, and the mysterious charm of the robbers' hideout.
