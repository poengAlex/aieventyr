The image style to depict this story would feature:

1. **Gradient Shading**: The use of smooth transitions between shades to convey the wintry scenes and the magical transformations, emphasizing the enchanted snowy forest and ethereal appearances.

2. **Clean Linework**: Precise, clear outlines accentuate the story's fairy-tale elements, portraying the queen, the princess, and the wild ducks with elegance.

3. **Vibrant Palette**: Utilizing a vivid color scheme to highlight the contrast between the white snow and red blood, the lush greenery of the forest, and the opulent regal attire worn by characters.

4. **Stylized Proportions**: Exaggerating certain features, like the wispy wands of the witch or the majestic feathers of the wild ducks, to inject a whimsical, larger-than-life quality into the magical elements of the story.

5. **Atmospheric Lighting**: Offering a sense of otherworldliness to the settings, such as the eerie glow of a moonlit night or the warm welcome of the cabin in the forest.

6. **Smooth Textures**: Creating a dreamy, fairy-tale aesthetic that complements the narrative, blending elements like snow drifts and flowing dresses seamlessly into the background.

This combination of artistic choices would beautifully capture the enchanting and fantastical nature of the tale, inviting viewers into a visually rich storybook world.
