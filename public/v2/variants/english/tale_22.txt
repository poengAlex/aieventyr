The Rooster and the Hen

Hen: "You promise me shoes, year after year, year after year, and I get no shoes!"

Rooster: "You will indeed get shoes!"

Hen: "I lay eggs, and I do well, and yet I must go barefoot here!"

Rooster: "Then take your eggs and travel to the city and buy yourself shoes, and no longer walk barefoot!"

In this children's fairy tale, the emphasis does everything, as the sounds of the rooster and the hen are mimicked.
