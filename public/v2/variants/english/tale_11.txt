Everyone thinks best of their own children.

Once upon a time there was a Hunter out in the Forest; and there he met the Snipe.

«Oh dear, do not shoot my children!» said the Snipe.

«And who are your children, then?» asked the Hunter.

«The most beautiful children in the Forest are mine!» answered the Snipe.

«Well, I guess I won't shoot them then,» said the Hunter.

But when he came back, he held in his hand a whole bundle of Snipe chicks that he had shot.

«Oh, oh! Why did you shoot my children after all!» said the Snipe.

«Were these your children?» asked the Hunter — «I shot the ugliest ones I found.»

«Oh yes!» responded the Snipe, «don't you know, that everyone thinks the best of their own children?»
