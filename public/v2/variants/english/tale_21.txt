The Smith whom they dared not let into Hell.  
Once in the days when our Lord and St. Peter were walking on Earth, they came to a Smith. He had made a pact with the Devil, in which he should belong to him after seven years, in return for becoming master above all masters in the art of smithery during that time; and both he and the Devil had signed their names under this contract. Therefore, he had also written in large letters above the smith's door: "Here lives the Master above all Masters!" When our Lord came and saw this, he went in.  
"Who are you?" he said to the smith.  
"Read above the door," replied the Smith; "but perhaps you can not read the inscription, so you must wait until someone comes who can help you."  
Before our Lord could answer him, a man came with a horse, which he asked the smith to shoe for him.  
"May I be permitted to shoe it?" said our Lord.  
"You can try," said the Smith; "worse you cannot make it, than I can make it right again."
