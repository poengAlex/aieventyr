12. A Suitor's Tale

Once upon a time, there was a young man who was out courting. Among other places, he also visited a farm where the people lived in utter poverty and destitution; but when the suitor arrived, they wanted to appear wealthy, that much anyone could tell.

The man had gotten a new sleeve in his tunic. "Sit down!" he said to the suitor. "But it's so dusty everywhere here!" and then he went and rubbed and wiped the benches and tables with the new sleeve of his tunic; he held his other arm behind his back.

The woman had received a new shoe; with that, she went about buckling and kicking at everything, stools and chairs; "there’s so much in the way, it's so untidy here!" she said.

Then they called to the daughter to come and clean up. She had gotten a new bonnet; therefore, she stuck her head through the door and nodded here and there. "I can't be everywhere either!" she said.

Ah yes, it was indeed affluent people the suitor had come to visit there.
