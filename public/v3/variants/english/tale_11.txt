Everyone Thinks Their Own Children Are Best

There was once a hunter who was out in the forest; there he met a snipe.

“My dear friend, please don’t shoot my children!” said the snipe.

“Which ones are your children, then?” asked the hunter.

“The most beautiful children in the forest are mine!” answered the snipe.

“Then I suppose I shouldn’t shoot them,” said the hunter.

But when he came back, he was holding a whole bunch of snipe chicks that he had shot.

“Ow, ow! Why did you shoot my children anyway?” asked the snipe.

“Those were yours?” said the hunter. “I shot the ugliest ones I found.”

“Oh yes!” replied the snipe, “Don’t you know that everyone thinks their own children are best?”
