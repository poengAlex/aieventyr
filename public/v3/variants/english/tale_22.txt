The Rooster and the Hen

Hen: “You promise me shoes, year after year, year after year,  
and I still have no shoes!”

Rooster: “You shall certainly have shoes!”

Hen: “I lay eggs, and I do good, and yet I must wander around barefoot!”

Rooster: “Then take your eggs, go to the city,  
and buy yourself some shoes, so you need not go barefoot any longer!”
