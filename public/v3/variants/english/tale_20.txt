The Fox as Shepherd

Once upon a time, there was a woman who needed to go out and hire a shepherd. As she was walking, she met a Bear.

“Where are you going?” said the Bear.  

“Ah, I’m going out to hire a shepherd,” answered the woman.  

“Won’t you have me as your shepherd?” asked the Bear.  

“Yes, if only you can call the animals,” said the woman.  

“Hoo–ee!” said the Bear.  

“No, I won’t have you,” said the woman when she heard that, and went on her way.

After she had walked a bit further, she met a Wolf.  

“Where are you going?” said the Wolf.  

“I’m going out to hire a shepherd,” answered the woman.  

“Won’t you have me as your shepherd?” asked the Wolf.  

“Yes, if only you can call the animals,” said the woman.  

“Uhh–uhh!” said the Wolf.  

“No, I won’t have you,” said the woman.

She continued on her way and soon met a Fox.  

“Where are you going?” asked the Fox.  

“Ah, I’m going out to hire a shepherd,” said the woman.  

“Won’t you have me as your shepherd?” asked the Fox.  

“Yes, if only you can call the animals,” said the woman.  

“Dil–dal–holom!” said the Fox, so clear and fine.  

“Yes, I’ll have you as my shepherd,” said the woman, and so she set the Fox to watch over her livestock.

On the first day the Fox was shepherd, he ate all the woman’s goats. On the second day, he finished off all her sheep. And on the third day, he ate up all her cows.

When he came home in the evening, the woman asked where he had put all her animals.

“The bones are in the river and the bodies are in the forest,” said the Fox.

She was in the middle of churning butter, but she decided she had better go see for herself. While she was gone, the Fox sneaked down into the churn and ate up all the cream.

When the woman came back and saw what he had done, she became so angry that she took the little bit of cream that remained and threw it at the Fox. He got a splash on the end of his tail; that is why the Fox’s tail tip is white to this day.
