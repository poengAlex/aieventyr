12. A Suitor’s Tale

Once upon a time, there was a boy who went out courting.
Among other places, he also came to a farm where
the people lived in total poverty and hardship; but when the suitor
arrived, they naturally wanted to appear well off, as you can imagine.

The man had gotten a new sleeve on his jacket.
“Have a seat!” he said to the suitor. “But everything here
is so dusty!” And then he went around rubbing and wiping
the benches and tables with his new jacket sleeve,
while keeping his other arm behind his back.

The wife had gotten a new shoe; with it, she went around
stretching and kicking everything in sight—stools and chairs.
“There’s so much clutter here; it’s so untidy!”
she said.

Then they called for their daughter, telling her to come
and tidy up. She had gotten a new cap; so she stuck
her head through the doorway and nodded here and there.
“I can’t be everywhere at once, can I?” she said.

Oh yes, indeed it was a well-to-do household the suitor
had come to there.
