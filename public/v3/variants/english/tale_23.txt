The Rooster, the Cuckoo, and the Capercaillie

The Rooster, the Cuckoo, and the Capercaillie had once bought
a Cow together. Since they could not split it in a fair way,
and they could not agree on buying each other out,
they decided that whoever woke up first in the morning
would get the Cow. Then the Rooster woke up first.

“Now the Cow is mine!
Now the Cow is mine!” said the Rooster.

When the Rooster crowed, the Cuckoo woke up.
“Half a Cow!
Half a Cow!” said the Cuckoo.

When he crowed, the Capercaillie woke up.
“Dear brothers, share
as right and reason demand,
right and reason demand! Tsio! tsi!” said the Capercaillie.
Can you then tell me who ought to have the Cow?
