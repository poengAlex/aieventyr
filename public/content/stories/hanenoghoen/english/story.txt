The Rooster and the Hen

The Hen said, “You promise me shoes year after year, year after year, and I never get any shoes!”

The Rooster said, “You’ll get shoes soon enough!”

The Hen said, “I lay eggs, and I do my part, and still I have to go around here barefoot!”

The Rooster said, “Then take your eggs and go to town and buy yourself shoes, and don’t go barefoot anymore!”
