The Hen stamped a bare, dusty foot.

“You promise me shoes year after year, year after year,” she clucked, “and I never get any shoes!”

The Rooster puffed out his chest. “You’ll have your shoes, you’ll see!”

The Hen hugged her basket of eggs.

“I lay eggs, and I earn my keep, and still I have to go barefoot!”

The Rooster waved a wing down the road.

“Then take your eggs to town and buy yourself some shoes,” he crowed, “and stop going barefoot!”
