Once upon a time there was a lad who set out to go courting. He called at many farms, and at one of them the people were as poor as church mice. But when a suitor comes calling, folk like to look well-off. Anyone can understand that.

The Farmer had got a new sleeve on his sweater.

“Sit down, sit down!” he said, and the Suitor perched on the bench. “Goodness, the dust in here!”

And the Farmer went round rubbing and wiping the benches and the table with his brand-new sleeve. The other arm he kept behind his back.

The Farmer’s Wife had got one new shoe. With it she went about kicking and shoving at everything, stools and chairs alike.

“Everything’s in the way here!” she said. “What a mess!”

Then they called for the Daughter to come and tidy up. The Daughter had got a new cap. So she only put her head round the door and nodded this way and that.

“I can’t be everywhere at once, can I?” she said.

Oh yes, the Suitor had come to very well-to-do people indeed.
