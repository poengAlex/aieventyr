Once there was a hunter who was out in the forest, and there he met a little snipe.

“Dear friend, don’t shoot my children!” said the snipe.

“And what kind are your children, then?” the hunter asked.

“The prettiest children that walk in the whole forest are mine!” the snipe answered.

“Then I suppose I mustn’t shoot them,” said the hunter.

But when he came back, he had in his hand a whole bunch of young snipe he had shot.

“Oh, oh! Why did you shoot my children anyway?” cried the snipe.

“Were these yours?” the hunter asked. “I only shot the ugliest ones I could find.”

“Oh yes,” said the snipe. “Don’t you know that everyone thinks their own children are the best?”
