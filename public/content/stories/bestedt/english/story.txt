Once upon a time a hunter out in the forest met the Snipe. She fluttered up in front of him, wings spread wide.

“Please,” she said, “don’t shoot my children!”

The Hunter stopped. “And which ones are your children?”

“The prettiest children that walk in the forest are mine!” said the Snipe. She was quite sure of it.

“Well then, I suppose I mustn’t shoot them,” said the Hunter.

But when he came back, he had a whole bunch of young snipes dangling from his hand. He had shot them.

“Oh! Oh!” cried the Snipe. “Why did you shoot my children after all?”

The Hunter held one up, calm as you like. “Were these yours?” he said. “I only shot the ugliest ones I could find.”

The Snipe lifted her head.

“Oh yes,” she said. “Don’t you know that everyone loves their own children best?”
