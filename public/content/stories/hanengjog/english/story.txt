The Rooster, the Cuckoo, and the Year-Rooster had once bought a cow together. But since there was no way to divide it, and they could not agree on buying one another out, they settled on this: whoever woke first in the morning should have the cow.

So the Rooster woke first.

“Now the cow is mine!
Now the cow is mine!” said the Rooster.

When the Rooster crowed, the Cuckoo woke.

“Half a cow!
Half a cow!” said the Cuckoo.

When he called, the Year-Rooster woke.

“My dear brothers, share as is right and fair—
Right and fair! Tsio! tsi!” said the Year-Rooster.

Now can you tell me who ought to have the cow?
