The Rooster, the Cuckoo and the Black Grouse once bought a cow together. But you can’t split a cow three ways, and none of them would give in. In the end they agreed: whoever woke first in the morning would get the Cow.

It was the Rooster’s idea.

So the Rooster woke first. He flew up onto the fence and crowed:

“Now the cow is mine! Now the cow is mine!”

The Cow chewed her cud.

When the Rooster crowed, the Cuckoo woke.

“Half-cow! Half-cow!” called the Cuckoo.

The Rooster bristled. “Now the cow is mine!”

“Half-cow!”

When the Cuckoo called, the Black Grouse woke. He stepped between them and raised a wing:

“My dear brothers, share and share alike, share and share alike! Tsio! Tsi!”

Now can you tell me who should have the cow?
