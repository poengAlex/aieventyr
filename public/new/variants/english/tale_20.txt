The Fox as a Shepard.

Once upon a time there was a woman who was going to hire a shepherd. Then she met a bear.

"Where are you going?" the bear asked.

"Oh, I am going to hire a shepherd," she replied.

"Don't you want me as a shepherd?" asked the bear. 

"Well, as long as you can call the animals," she replied. 

"Ho-il," said the bear.

"No, I don't want you," replied the woman, upon hearing it, and she continued on her way. 

When she had walked a little further, she met a wolf. 

"Where are you going?" asked the wolf.

"I am going to hire a shepherd," she replied.

"Don't you want me as a shepherd?" asked the wolf.

"Yes, if you can call the animals," she replied.

"Uh-uh!" said the wolf.

"No, I don't want you either," said the woman.

Having walked a bit further, she came across a fox.

"Where are you going?" asked the fox.

"Oh, I am going to hire a shepherd," she replied.

"Don't you want me as a shepherd?" asked the fox.

"Yes, as long as you can call the animals," she replied.

"Dil-dal-holom!" said the fox, rather grandly and finely.

"Yes, I will have you as a shepherd," she declared, and so, she put the fox in charge of herding her livestock.

On his first day as a shepherd, the fox ate all of her goats. The second day, he finished off all her sheep, and on the third day, he ate up all her cows. When he came home in the evening, the woman asked where he had put all her livestock. "The skins are in the river and the bodies are in the forest," said the fox. She was just churning butter but felt she had to go look for them. While she was gone, the fox sneaked into the churn and ate up the cream. When she came back and saw what happened, she got so angry that she took the little ladle of cream that was left and threw it at the fox, so he got a splash on the end of his tail. Therefore, it is said that the fox has a white tip on his tail.
