Once upon a time, Hanen, Gjøgen, and Aarhanen bought a cow together. Since it wasn't possible to divide the cow, and they couldn't agree on buying each other out, they agreed that the one who woke up first in the morning should have the cow. So, Hanen woke up first. 
"The cow is mine! The cow is mine!" said Hanen. 
When Hanen crowed, Gjøgen woke up. 
"Half a cow! Half a cow!" said Gjøgen. 
When he crowed, Aarhanen woke up. 
"Dear brothers, divide as right and wrong, right and wrong! Tsio! Tsi!" said Aarhanen, can you then tell me, who should have the cow?
