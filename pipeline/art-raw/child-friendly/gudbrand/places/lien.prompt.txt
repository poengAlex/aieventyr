Place: the Hillside Farm. A tiny farm high on a steep, snowy hillside: a low, dark-brown log cottage with a thick, snow-covered turf roof, a stone chimney, two small four-paned windows and a sturdy plank door painted dusky blue, with a little cowshed beside it. A birch and a rowan tree with red berries stand in the yard next to a sheaf of oats on a tall pole, and a narrow path zigzags steeply down towards the valley far below.

Story world: Old rural Norway, long ago, in a deep mountain valley in the week before Christmas. Gudbrand's small farm sits high up on a steep hillside: a low log cottage and a little cowshed. A narrow path zigzags down past the Neighbour's farm to a small market town by a frozen river, along a snowy road lined with birches and sheaves of oats on poles. Gudbrand and his wife have three small children: a boy of about eight with short black hair and a green jumper, a girl of about six with two stubby fair plaits and a red dress, and a chubby toddler in a long white shirt. The animals: a black cow with a white face, a sandy-yellow fjord horse with a striped pale mane, a fat pink pig, a white nanny goat, a woolly white sheep, a big white goose and a cinnamon-brown rooster.

This story's season, light and colours: The week before Christmas in clear, frosty weather: deep snow on the steep hillside, the fields and the turf roofs, short days of pale lemon-yellow sunshine with long blue shadows, and a pink glow when the sun goes down. Then a clear, deep-indigo night full of stars, with every small window glowing candle-yellow. Inside the cottage: warm firelight, straw ornaments and red-and-white cloths. Accents: the Wife's cornflower-blue apron and bright red rowan berries.

Art style: Warm Scandinavian children's picture-book illustration: gouache and watercolour on lightly textured cold-press paper, with soft coloured-pencil outlines and visible brush and paper texture. Rounded, friendly shapes and simple, expressive faces with clear emotions, but every character has their own face shape, nose, eyes, hair, build and age exactly as described, so no two characters look alike. Base palette of moss green, spruce green, lingonberry red, birch white, fjord teal, honey yellow and warm earth browns, shifted towards each story's own season, light and colours. The pictures are the colour plates of a classic printed storybook with warm cream pages, so the lightest tones are warm and creamy, never stark white, and lingonberry red is the accent that comes back in every picture. The picture fills the whole image, with no border, frame or lettering. Costumes and places from old rural Norway: spruce and birch forests, fjords and mountains, turf-roofed log houses, carved wood, rosemaling patterns, wool and linen clothes. Kings and princesses wear simple storybook finery. Trolls are huge, lumpy and mossy and look silly rather than scary. Clear, uncluttered composition with one focal point and room to breathe. Cosy, safe and full of wonder. Not photorealistic, not 3D, not anime, no glossy digital look.
