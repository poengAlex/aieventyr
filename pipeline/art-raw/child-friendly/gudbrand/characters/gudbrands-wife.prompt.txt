A narrow path zigzags down past the Neighbour's farm to a small market town by a frozen river, along a snowy road lined with birches and sheaves of oats on poles. Gudbrand and his wife have three small children: a boy of about eight with short black hair and a green jumper, a girl of about six with two stubby fair plaits and a red dress, and a chubby toddler in a long white shirt.
