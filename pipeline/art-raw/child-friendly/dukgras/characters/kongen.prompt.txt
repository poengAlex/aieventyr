The king's castle is a large palace of dark logs with grass-covered turf roofs, carved dragon gables and a wide courtyard, among blooming meadows. In a meadow by the road stands the grassy tussock where the tiny maiden lives: a round, knee-high hump of grass and clover that opens like a little door. Inside is a snug, round room with walls of woven roots, a tiny chair, a tiny spinning wheel and a tiny loom. The tiny shirt is snow-white linen with perfect little stitches, no bigger than a man's palm. Her carriage is an ordinary shiny silver tablespoon pulled by two small white mice with reins of red thread. The big lake by the road is wide and still, with reeds and water lilies. Art style: Warm Scandinavian children's picture-book illustration: gouache and watercolour on lightly textured cold-press paper, with soft coloured-pencil outlines and visible brush and paper texture.
