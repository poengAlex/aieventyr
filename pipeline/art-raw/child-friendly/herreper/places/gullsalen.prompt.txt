Place: the Golden Hall. A great hall inside the castle where everything is made of gold: golden walls carved with swirling rosemaling patterns, a long golden table with golden chairs, golden candlesticks, plates and cups, and even golden door handles. Tall arched windows look out towards the three gates, a wide golden fireplace stands at one end, and the floor is polished golden wood.

Story world: Old rural Norway, long ago. Per's parents' tiny grey cottage stands empty at the edge of a spruce forest. The king's farm is a cluster of red-painted log buildings with turf roofs, a great log hall and a busy kitchen full of copper pots. A long country road winds from there over green hills, past pastures with sheep, cows and horses, to the Glittering Castle, where everything inside is gold. The pot is a round black iron pot, the griddle a big, flat, round iron griddle with a handle, and the cat's little gift box is tied with a red bow. The shining carriage is a small golden coach drawn by four white horses. The golden scissors are small and fine.

This story's season, light and colours: Bright high summer: deep-blue skies with fat white clouds, lush green hills and meadows full of buttercups, and dazzling sunshine that makes every silver and gold thing sparkle. The one night at the castle is deep violet-blue with warm golden candlelight, and the dawn that ends it glows peach and rose over the ridge. Accent colours: dove-grey, scarlet and glittering gold.

Art style: Warm Scandinavian children's picture-book illustration: gouache and watercolour on lightly textured cold-press paper, with soft coloured-pencil outlines and visible brush and paper texture. Rounded, friendly shapes and simple, expressive faces with clear emotions, but every character has their own face shape, nose, eyes, hair, build and age exactly as described, so no two characters look alike. Base palette of moss green, spruce green, lingonberry red, birch white, fjord teal, honey yellow and warm earth browns, shifted towards each story's own season, light and colours. Cosy, safe and full of wonder. Not photorealistic, not 3D, not anime, no glossy digital look.
