Character: the Dressed-up Cat. The same clever grey tabby cat with dark silver-grey stripes, four snow-white paws, a white chest, big green-gold eyes and her old faded red leather collar, now dressed up like Per: a tiny scarlet cape fastened at her throat and a little green hat with a curling white feather perched jauntily between her ears. She sits upright and proud, with her striped tail held high.

Story world: Old rural Norway, long ago. Per's parents' tiny grey cottage stands empty at the edge of a spruce forest. The king's farm is a cluster of red-painted log buildings with turf roofs, a great log hall and a busy kitchen full of copper pots. A long country road winds from there over green hills, past pastures with sheep, cows and horses, to the Glittering Castle, where everything inside is gold. The pot is a round black iron pot, the griddle a big, flat, round iron griddle with a handle, and the cat's little gift box is tied with a red bow. The shining carriage is a small golden coach drawn by four white horses. The golden scissors are small and fine.

This story's season, light and colours: Bright high summer: deep-blue skies with fat white clouds, lush green hills and meadows full of buttercups, and dazzling sunshine that makes every silver and gold thing sparkle. The one night at the castle is deep violet-blue with warm golden candlelight, and the dawn that ends it glows peach and rose over the ridge. Accent colours: dove-grey, scarlet and glittering gold.

Art style: Warm Scandinavian children's picture-book illustration: gouache and watercolour on lightly textured cold-press paper, with soft coloured-pencil outlines and visible brush and paper texture. Rounded, friendly shapes and simple, expressive faces with clear emotions, but every character has their own face shape, nose, eyes, hair, build and age exactly as described, so no two characters look alike. Base palette of moss green, spruce green, lingonberry red, birch white, fjord teal, honey yellow and warm earth browns, shifted towards each story's own season, light and colours. The pictures are the colour plates of a classic printed storybook with warm cream pages, so the lightest tones are warm and creamy, never stark white, and lingonberry red is the accent that comes back in every picture. The picture fills the whole image, with no border, frame or lettering. Costumes and places from old rural Norway: spruce and birch forests, fjords and mountains, turf-roofed log houses, carved wood, rosemaling patterns, wool and linen clothes. Kings and princesses wear simple storybook finery. Trolls are huge, lumpy and mossy and look silly rather than scary. Clear, uncluttered composition with one focal point and room to breathe. Cosy, safe and full of wonder. Not photorealistic, not 3D, not anime, no glossy digital look.
