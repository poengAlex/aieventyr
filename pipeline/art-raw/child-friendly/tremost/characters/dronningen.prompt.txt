Character: the Queen. A tall, willowy, kindly queen of about fifty with a long neck, a long oval face, crinkly grey eyes, a slightly long nose and dark hair streaked with silver in a low, coiled knot under a small gold crown. She wears a sea-green gown with a frilled cream collar and a long rainbow-striped knitted shawl, and she is always knitting with two long wooden needles, a half-finished striped scarf and a ball of pink yarn.

Story world: Old rural Norway, long ago. A poor hunter lives in a small log cabin deep in a spruce forest. The royal farm is a big, friendly cluster of honey-brown log buildings with grass roofs, carved gables and small-paned windows around a cobbled yard with a well. The flax is soft, pale-gold fibre in a big wicker basket; spun, it becomes smooth, shiny golden yarn wound into soft balls; woven, it becomes a thick roll of fine cream-white linen; sewn, it becomes a neat pile of white linen shirts with shiny buttons. The Queen's servant girls are three young women in grey dresses, white aprons and white caps. The wedding is held in the royal farm's great hall, with six guests in festive clothes.

This story's season, light and colours: A long rainy spell at the end of summer: silver rain streaming down small-paned windows, dripping gardens in deep, wet greens and soft grey-violet skies, then fresh, rain-washed mornings with sunshine sparkling on puddles and wet leaves. Indoors: warm candle and lamp light on flax-gold yarn, cream linen and soft wool colours. The wedding glows with candlelight, bowls of pink and white garden flowers and a tall cake. Accents: flax gold and the Queen's rainbow-striped knitting.

Art style: Warm Scandinavian children's picture-book illustration: gouache and watercolour on lightly textured cold-press paper, with soft coloured-pencil outlines and visible brush and paper texture. Rounded, friendly shapes and simple, expressive faces with clear emotions, but every character has their own face shape, nose, eyes, hair, build and age exactly as described, so no two characters look alike. Base palette of moss green, spruce green, lingonberry red, birch white, fjord teal, honey yellow and warm earth browns, shifted towards each story's own season, light and colours. The pictures are the colour plates of a classic printed storybook with warm cream pages, so the lightest tones are warm and creamy, never stark white, and lingonberry red is the accent that comes back in every picture. The picture fills the whole image, with no border, frame or lettering. Costumes and places from old rural Norway: spruce and birch forests, fjords and mountains, turf-roofed log houses, carved wood, rosemaling patterns, wool and linen clothes. Kings and princesses wear simple storybook finery. Trolls are huge, lumpy and mossy and look silly rather than scary. Clear, uncluttered composition with one focal point and room to breathe. Cosy, safe and full of wonder. Not photorealistic, not 3D, not anime, no glossy digital look.
