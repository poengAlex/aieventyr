Picture: Night in the candlelit Little Chamber. The Girl sits at the spinning wheel with a huge, hopeless tangle of pale-gold flax in her lap and looks up in surprise. The door has creaked open, and three curious old faces peek in around it, one above the other: the Long-Nosed Aunt at the top with her enormous nose poking in first, the Teary-Eyed Aunt in the middle dabbing her huge eyes, and the Broad-Hipped Aunt at the bottom, her wide striped skirt filling the doorway. Rain streaks the window.

Characters in this picture (nobody else, unless the picture description mentions them):
- the Girl: A slim, cheerful hunter's daughter of about sixteen with an oval face dusted all over with freckles, a snub nose, big hazel eyes, a wide smile and mousy-brown hair in one thick plait over her shoulder. She wears a plain pine-green linen dress with the sleeves rolled up, a short brown sheepskin vest, a leather belt and sturdy brown shoes, and has a blue jay feather tucked behind one ear.
- the Long-Nosed Aunt: A small, slim, spry old woman of about eighty who holds herself upright, with a kindly, wrinkled face, merry little blue eyes, rosy cheeks and an enormous nose as long and thick as a cucumber, poking far out in front of her face with a round pink tip. - the Teary-Eyed Aunt: A round-shouldered old woman of about eighty, of middle height, with a soft, wrinkled face and huge round eyes as big as saucers, pink-rimmed and always brimming with tears, so she keeps dabbing them with a big white lace handkerchief. - the Broad-Hipped Aunt: A short old woman of about eighty with narrow shoulders, a small round head and enormously wide hips, so wide that her skirt looks like a haystack. She has a square little face, a snub nose, bright squinting eyes, a gap-toothed grin and grey hair in a tight bun under a small round black felt hat. She wears a huge, bell-shaped skirt with wide red and ochre stripes, a white blouse and a short dark-brown knitted jacket.
