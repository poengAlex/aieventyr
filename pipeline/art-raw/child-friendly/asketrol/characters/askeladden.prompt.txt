Character: Askeladden. A small, wiry boy of about eleven, shorter than most boys his age, with a round face, rosy cheeks, bright brown eyes, a button nose, a dark-brown curly mop of hair and a cheeky gap-toothed grin. He wears a moss-green knitted jumper with brown leather elbow patches, dark-brown knee breeches, thick oatmeal-coloured wool stockings and scuffed brown boots. A small brown linen sack with a drawstring hangs from his shoulder.

Story world: Old rural Norway, long ago. A poor old farmer lives with his three sons in a small, low log cottage with a turf roof at the edge of a deep forest of spruce and birch. The troll lives inside a steep, rocky mountain in the middle of the forest, behind a heavy round wooden door. Everything in his home is troll-sized: two iron buckets as big as bathtubs, a huge black iron pot over the fire, a porridge spoon as big as a spade, giant wooden bowls and a long plank table with one long bench. Askeladden's cheese is small, round, soft and white, and fits in his hand. His sack is a small brown linen sack with a drawstring. The brothers' axes are ordinary wood axes with short handles.

This story's season, light and colours: Misty autumn in the forest: rust-red and orange birch leaves, dark spruces, golden ferns and cool grey mist drifting between the trunks in soft, pale daylight. Inside the mountain: warm amber firelight on rough brown rock. At home in the cottage: pale daylight through one small window, and in the evening the glow of a single candle.

Art style: Warm Scandinavian children's picture-book illustration: gouache and watercolour on lightly textured cold-press paper, with soft coloured-pencil outlines and visible brush and paper texture. Rounded, friendly shapes and simple, expressive faces with clear emotions, but every character has their own face shape, nose, eyes, hair, build and age exactly as described, so no two characters look alike. Base palette of moss green, spruce green, lingonberry red, birch white, fjord teal, honey yellow and warm earth browns, shifted towards each story's own season, light and colours. The pictures are the colour plates of a classic printed storybook with warm cream pages, so the lightest tones are warm and creamy, never stark white, and lingonberry red is the accent that comes back in every picture. The picture fills the whole image, with no border, frame or lettering. Costumes and places from old rural Norway: spruce and birch forests, fjords and mountains, turf-roofed log houses, carved wood, rosemaling patterns, wool and linen clothes. Kings and princesses wear simple storybook finery. Trolls are huge, lumpy and mossy and look silly rather than scary. Clear, uncluttered composition with one focal point and room to breathe. Cosy, safe and full of wonder. Not photorealistic, not 3D, not anime, no glossy digital look.

Rules:
