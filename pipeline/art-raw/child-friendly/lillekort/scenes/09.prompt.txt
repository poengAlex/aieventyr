Create one new illustration for a children's picture book, showing the moment described below.

Picture: Night on the Shore. The Five-Headed Troll wades out of a black ship with black sails through foaming waves, so huge that its barnacled hump blots out the big orange moon, and all five heads peer down curiously. Tiny Lillekort has just run onto the sand, the golden hilt of his sword glinting at his belt; he plants his feet and looks up with a bold grin. The Youngest Princess clings to her stone, and high in the oak Sir Red's wide eyes peek out between the leaves.

Characters in this picture (nobody else, unless the picture description mentions them):
- Lillekort: A tiny, bold little boy, no bigger than a four-year-old child, with a round face, rosy cheeks, a small button nose, big bright cornflower-blue eyes, a determined chin and a mop of golden curls. On his head sits an oversized knitted cap with blue-and-white stripes, flopping over one ear. He wears his big brothers' old rags: a huge, faded blue linen smock covered in patches, hanging to his knees like a nightshirt and tied at the waist with a piece of rope, and enormous, floppy old leather boots.
- the Five-Headed Troll: A huge, hunched sea troll five times as tall as a man, with dusky purple-grey skin like a mussel shell and a round hump of a back like an upturned boat, crusted with barnacles and seaweed. Exactly five heads poke out in a row from under the front of the hump, like turtles peeping out of a shell, each with a long drooping nose, small round yellow eyes and shaggy grey hair. It has long, dangling arms, flat flipper feet and an old fishing net knotted round its middle.
- the Youngest Princess: A slim, lively princess of about sixteen with a small, delicate face, a little pointed chin, a tiny snub nose, big grey eyes, pink cheeks and short, tight platinum-blonde curls cropped close like a lamb's fleece. On her curls sits a small gold crown shaped like a ring of oak leaves. She wears an emerald-green velvet dress with puffed shoulders, narrow sleeves and a high round collar embroidered with tiny gold leaves, a thin gold belt, and a string of small white pearls around her neck.
- Sir Red: A big, burly, barrel-chested knight of about forty, a head taller than most men, with a broad, puffy face that easily turns bright red, a big bulbous nose, small shifty pale-blue eyes under bushy brows, curly red hair and a huge, bushy fiery-red beard. He wears polished steel armour that clanks with every step over a red tunic, a long bright-red cloak and heavy steel-capped boots, and always has a big white handkerchief tucked into his belt.

Setting:
- the Shore: A wide, pale sandy beach below the royal farm, curving along the open sea. Right at the water's edge lies one big, flat, grey boulder, and at the top of the beach stands a huge, gnarled old oak with wide, spreading branches and yellow-brown autumn leaves. Tufts of beach grass grow in the low dunes, and a grassy slope rises behind to the dark log buildings of the royal farm.

Story world: Old Norway, long ago, in late autumn. Lillekort is born in a tiny, crooked cottage with a sagging turf roof at the edge of a forest of yellow birches and red rowans. Far away, the royal farm stands on a slope above a sandy shore by the open sea: dark log buildings with turf roofs around a long hall, all hung with black cloth at first. Lillekort's sword has a shining golden hilt and hangs in a plain brown leather sheath. His magic ship is a wooden sailing ship with two masts and white sails; when it is small, it is no bigger than a slipper and fits in his pocket. The sea trolls come in black ships with black sails, and their hair is grey. The princess's three secret suits for Lillekort are of gold, silver and brass. The iron chain is huge, heavy and five hundred metres long.

This story's season, light and colours: Late autumn on the open coast: crisp, blustery days with yellow birch leaves and scarlet rowan berries whirling over a dark-green sea with white-capped waves. On the three Thursday nights a huge, low, orange harvest moon hangs over the shore and silver sea mist drifts across the sand. The royal farm is draped in black until the wedding, when candlelight makes gold, silver and brass glitter. Under the sea: cool green-blue water and the red-orange glow of the troll's brewing fire.

Art style: Warm Scandinavian children's picture-book illustration: gouache and watercolour on lightly textured cold-press paper, with soft coloured-pencil outlines and visible brush and paper texture. Rounded, friendly shapes and simple, expressive faces with clear emotions, but every character has their own face shape, nose, eyes, hair, build and age exactly as described, so no two characters look alike. Base palette of moss green, spruce green, lingonberry red, birch white, fjord teal, honey yellow and warm earth browns, shifted towards each story's own season, light and colours. The pictures are the colour plates of a classic printed storybook with warm cream pages, so the lightest tones are warm and creamy, never stark white, and lingonberry red is the accent that comes back in every picture. The picture fills the whole image, with no border, frame or lettering. Costumes and places from old rural Norway: spruce and birch forests, fjords and mountains, turf-roofed log houses, carved wood, rosemaling patterns, wool and linen clothes. Kings and princesses wear simple storybook finery. Trolls are huge, lumpy and mossy and look silly rather than scary. Clear, uncluttered composition with one focal point and room to breathe. Cosy, safe and full of wonder. Not photorealistic, not 3D, not anime, no glossy digital look.
