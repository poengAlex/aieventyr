Character: Lavring in Gold. The tiny, bold twin brother dressed in his twin's clothes, so that nobody can tell the two apart: no bigger than a four-year-old child, with a round face, rosy cheeks, a small button nose, big bright cornflower-blue eyes and a mop of golden curls under an oversized blue-and-white striped knitted cap. He wears a shining suit of pure gold: a short gold jacket with round gold buttons and a stiff little collar, gold knee breeches, gold stockings and small gold shoes.

Story world: Old Norway, long ago, in late autumn. Lillekort is born in a tiny, crooked cottage with a sagging turf roof at the edge of a forest of yellow birches and red rowans. Far away, the royal farm stands on a slope above a sandy shore by the open sea: dark log buildings with turf roofs around a long hall, all hung with black cloth at first. Lillekort's sword has a shining golden hilt and hangs in a plain brown leather sheath. His magic ship is a wooden sailing ship with two masts and white sails; when it is small, it is no bigger than a slipper and fits in his pocket. The sea trolls come in black ships with black sails, and their hair is grey. The princess's three secret suits for Lillekort are of gold, silver and brass. The iron chain is huge, heavy and five hundred metres long.

This story's season, light and colours: Late autumn on the open coast: crisp, blustery days with yellow birch leaves and scarlet rowan berries whirling over a dark-green sea with white-capped waves. On the three Thursday nights a huge, low, orange harvest moon hangs over the shore and silver sea mist drifts across the sand. The royal farm is draped in black until the wedding, when candlelight makes gold, silver and brass glitter. Under the sea: cool green-blue water and the red-orange glow of the troll's brewing fire.

Art style: Warm Scandinavian children's picture-book illustration: gouache and watercolour on lightly textured cold-press paper, with soft coloured-pencil outlines and visible brush and paper texture. Rounded, friendly shapes and simple, expressive faces with clear emotions, but every character has their own face shape, nose, eyes, hair, build and age exactly as described, so no two characters look alike. Base palette of moss green, spruce green, lingonberry red, birch white, fjord teal, honey yellow and warm earth browns, shifted towards each story's own season, light and colours. The pictures are the colour plates of a classic printed storybook with warm cream pages, so the lightest tones are warm and creamy, never stark white, and lingonberry red is the accent that comes back in every picture. The picture fills the whole image, with no border, frame or lettering. Costumes and places from old rural Norway: spruce and birch forests, fjords and mountains, turf-roofed log houses, carved wood, rosemaling patterns, wool and linen clothes. Kings and princesses wear simple storybook finery. Trolls are huge, lumpy and mossy and look silly rather than scary. Clear, uncluttered composition with one focal point and room to breathe. Cosy, safe and full of wonder. Not photorealistic, not 3D, not anime, no glossy digital look.
