Story world: Old Norway by the sea, long ago, over fourteen years of voyages. The princesses' father rules a royal farm of big log halls above a sandy beach. On a small rocky island stands the troll prince's castle; far across the sea, a dark stone castle on a rocky shore holds the troll king's great hall of gold.
