Character: the King. A small, wiry, elderly king with a narrow, kind, crinkly face, a small sharp nose, twinkling blue eyes, wisps of silver hair poking out under his crown and a long silver-grey beard braided into two plaits tied with little gold rings. He wears a low, wide gold crown set with green stones, a long turquoise robe with a collar of spotted grey sealskin and silver clasps shaped like fish, and soft grey felt slippers.

Story world: Old Norway by the sea, long ago, over fourteen years of voyages. The princesses' father rules a royal farm of big log halls above a sandy beach. On a small rocky island stands the troll prince's castle; far across the sea, a dark stone castle on a rocky shore holds the troll king's great hall of gold. The little dog is fluffy and white, with floppy brown ears. The old sword has a plain iron hilt; the magic bottle is round and made of blue glass. The crown cupboard is waist-high, painted blue with red-and-white rosemaling, and holds twelve small golden crowns. The iron boat is small, dark and has no oars, with a heavy iron club inside. The eleven older princes wear matching grey-blue coats; the eleven older princesses wear long dresses in different soft colours.

This story's season, light and colours: Changeable weather on the open northern sea in spring, from gales to calm, bright days: jade-green and pewter-grey waves with white foam, towering silver-grey clouds and clear rosy dawns. Indoors, warm lamplight and the rich glow of polished gold. Accents: Ridder Rød's scarlet, the prince's forest green and gleaming golden crowns.

Art style: Warm Scandinavian children's picture-book illustration: gouache and watercolour on lightly textured cold-press paper, with soft coloured-pencil outlines and visible brush and paper texture. Rounded, friendly shapes and simple, expressive faces with clear emotions, but every character has their own face shape, nose, eyes, hair, build and age exactly as described, so no two characters look alike. Base palette of moss green, spruce green, lingonberry red, birch white, fjord teal, honey yellow and warm earth browns, shifted towards each story's own season, light and colours. The picture fills the whole image, with no border, frame or lettering. Costumes and places from old rural Norway: spruce and birch forests, fjords and mountains, turf-roofed log houses, carved wood, rosemaling patterns, wool and linen clothes. Kings and princesses wear simple storybook finery. Trolls are huge, lumpy and mossy and look silly rather than scary. Clear, uncluttered composition with one focal point and room to breathe. Cosy, safe and full of wonder. Not photorealistic, not 3D, not anime, no glossy digital look.
