Character: Kari in the Silver Gown. A slender girl of about fifteen with a fine-boned face, high cheekbones, a slim nose, wide-set hazel eyes under straight dark-copper brows, a dimple in her chin, fair skin and long, wavy copper hair in one loose braid woven with silver ribbons. She wears a long gown of shimmering silver cloth that glitters like frost, trimmed with rows of tiny silver leaves like little bells, a short silver-grey riding cape and silver slippers.

Story world: Old rural Norway, long ago. The princess grows up at her father's long, white-painted timber palace, with a red cowshed and forest pastures around it. The blue ox keeps a small white cloth in his left ear; spread on the grass, it fills itself with food. Far away lie three magic forests: one of copper, where every trunk and leaf is polished copper and clinks in the wind; one of silver, whose leaves tinkle like little bells; and one of gold, where gold drips from the leaves like honey. Each troll carries a curved cow horn of healing salve on a string at its belt. Beyond the forests, a steep rock wall looks down on the prince's huge castle of pale-grey stone with green copper roofs, with a small pigsty below it and a dark stave church nearby. The gold shoes are small, pointed and shining.

This story's season, light and colours: Cool, clear early autumn under high, pale-blue skies: blue-grey fells, silver birches just turning yellow and purple heather, with the ox's deep, glossy blue as the strongest colour until the magic forests blaze in polished copper, frosty silver and molten gold. The castle kitchen is dim, warm and sooty; the church Sundays are fresh and green.

Art style: Warm Scandinavian children's picture-book illustration: gouache and watercolour on lightly textured cold-press paper, with soft coloured-pencil outlines and visible brush and paper texture. Rounded, friendly shapes and simple, expressive faces with clear emotions, but every character has their own face shape, nose, eyes, hair, build and age exactly as described, so no two characters look alike. Base palette of moss green, spruce green, lingonberry red, birch white, fjord teal, honey yellow and warm earth browns, shifted towards each story's own season, light and colours. The picture fills the whole image, with no border, frame or lettering. Costumes and places from old rural Norway: spruce and birch forests, fjords and mountains, turf-roofed log houses, carved wood, rosemaling patterns, wool and linen clothes. Cosy, safe and full of wonder. Not photorealistic, not 3D, not anime, no glossy digital look.
