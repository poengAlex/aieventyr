Character: Halvor in Fine Clothes. A tall, lanky lad of about eighteen with a long face, a long straight nose, sleepy hazel eyes, a lopsided smile and messy auburn hair, now washed clean and dressed like a king's son: a royal-blue velvet coat with gold buttons and gold-embroidered cuffs, a white linen shirt with a frilled collar, a wide lingonberry-red sash, cream breeches, white stockings and shiny black buckled shoes.

Story world: Old Norway, long ago. Halvor's home is a tiny, poor turf-roofed log cottage on a hillside above the sea, with one low room, a big open stone hearth full of ashes and not a single chair. Far across the sea lies an empty green land where three troll castles stand far apart, each grander than the last, with light in every window. The troll sword in each castle is a big old sword with a plain iron hilt; the magic bottle is a small green glass bottle with a cork. The wishing ring is a chunky silver ring with one round blue stone; the ring left in its place is a small, plain gold band. The magic boots are cracked old brown leather boots. Halvor's horse is a skinny, shaggy dun horse with a drooping head. Soria Moria is a castle of glittering golden towers far beyond forests and mountains.

This story's season, light and colours: Early autumn, mostly long blue dusks and clear moonlit nights: deep ultramarine and indigo skies, silver moonlight, violet-blue shadows and warm golden light glittering from castle windows far away. Indoors, amber firelight gleams on gold and silver. Daylight is cool and silvery. Accents: the youngest princess's red and the rose-and-ember glow of the sunset over Halvor's home.

Art style: Warm Scandinavian children's picture-book illustration: gouache and watercolour on lightly textured cold-press paper, with soft coloured-pencil outlines and visible brush and paper texture. Rounded, friendly shapes and simple, expressive faces with clear emotions, but every character has their own face shape, nose, eyes, hair, build and age exactly as described, so no two characters look alike. Base palette of moss green, spruce green, lingonberry red, birch white, fjord teal, honey yellow and warm earth browns, shifted towards each story's own season, light and colours. The pictures are the colour plates of a classic printed storybook with warm cream pages, so the lightest tones are warm and creamy, never stark white, and lingonberry red is the accent that comes back in every picture. The picture fills the whole image, with no border, frame or lettering. Costumes and places from old rural Norway: spruce and birch forests, fjords and mountains, turf-roofed log houses, carved wood, rosemaling patterns, wool and linen clothes. Kings and princesses wear simple storybook finery. Trolls are huge, lumpy and mossy and look silly rather than scary. Clear, uncluttered composition with one focal point and room to breathe. Cosy, safe and full of wonder. Not photorealistic, not 3D, not anime, no glossy digital look.
