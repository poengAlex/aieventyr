Character: the Cow. A gentle, plump cow with a red-and-white spotted coat, long curved cream-coloured horns, big soft brown eyes with long lashes, a wet pink nose, a calm, friendly face, a swishing tail with a white tuft and pale hooves. A wooden milk bucket with a rope handle always hangs from one of her horns.

Story world: Old rural Norway, long ago. At home: a small grey log farmhouse with a turf roof, a round stone well in the long grass in front, and a tiny pig-house of rough grey planks with one small window. Deep down the well lies another world: a wide green meadow with a low fence of woven hazel twigs, a big old apple tree with a kind face in its bark, and a path to the troll hag's farm, a grassy mound with a round wooden door. Her one room is dug into the earth: stone walls, roots hanging from the ceiling, a thick beam, and a plank table with a candle and a bowl of mushrooms. Her low, dark cowshed holds three black cows. The three chests are small wooden chests with curved lids: one red, one green, and one blue with three little white crosses on its lid.

This story's season, light and colours: Two worlds. At home above the well: a grey, damp day under low clouds, with wet grass and muted greens, browns and greys. Down below the well: a dreamlike, sunlit world of everlasting summer, with jewel-bright green grass, a big, soft white-gold sun in a cloudless apricot-tinted sky, white clover and red apples. In the troll hag's earth house: dim, earthy browns lit by one candle. At the end, the pig-house glows with warm gold and pearly light. Accents: the Girl's blue headscarf and the red, green and blue chests.

Art style: Warm Scandinavian children's picture-book illustration: gouache and watercolour on lightly textured cold-press paper, with soft coloured-pencil outlines and visible brush and paper texture. Rounded, friendly shapes and simple, expressive faces with clear emotions, but every character has their own face shape, nose, eyes, hair, build and age exactly as described, so no two characters look alike. Base palette of moss green, spruce green, lingonberry red, birch white, fjord teal, honey yellow and warm earth browns, shifted towards each story's own season, light and colours. The pictures are the colour plates of a classic printed storybook with warm cream pages, so the lightest tones are warm and creamy, never stark white, and lingonberry red is the accent that comes back in every picture. The picture fills the whole image, with no border, frame or lettering. Costumes and places from old rural Norway: spruce and birch forests, fjords and mountains, turf-roofed log houses, carved wood, rosemaling patterns, wool and linen clothes. Kings and princesses wear simple storybook finery. Trolls are huge, lumpy and mossy and look silly rather than scary. Clear, uncluttered composition with one focal point and room to breathe. Cosy, safe and full of wonder. Not photorealistic, not 3D, not anime, no glossy digital look.

Rules:
