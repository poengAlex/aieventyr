Character: the Widower. A skinny, stooped widower of about fifty with a long, narrow, tired face, heavy-lidded eyes, a long crooked nose, hollow cheeks, a scruffy salt-and-pepper chin beard and thin dark hair combed flat. He wears a collarless off-white shirt, dark-blue braces, baggy grey trousers, a darned heather-brown knitted cardigan and worn leather slippers, and he always looks as if he longs for some peace and quiet.

Story world: Old rural Norway, long ago, in a small farming village of red and grey log farms with turf roofs, scattered among fields, woods and bogs. The widower's little red farmhouse has a low kitchen with a plank table, and next to it grows a field of tall, bushy green hemp. Beyond the village lies a big, dark spruce forest. The farms have sheep barns, goose houses and, at the rich farmer's, a stabbur: a dark timber storehouse on stilts, hung inside with sausages and hams and with shelves of round loaves. On Sunday morning everything happens at the Turnip Field above the bog. Giske's soot is black and sticky, made of tallow and chimney soot.

This story's season, light and colours: Early autumn at hemp-pulling time: tall green-gold hemp, pale stubble fields and birches just turning yellow under a hazy blue sky. The three raids happen on dark, starry nights lit only by a thin moon and one warm lantern, and Sunday morning is fresh and dewy, with pearl-grey mist over the brown bog. Accent colours: sooty black, turnip purple and Giske's plum.

Art style: Warm Scandinavian children's picture-book illustration: gouache and watercolour on lightly textured cold-press paper, with soft coloured-pencil outlines and visible brush and paper texture. Rounded, friendly shapes and simple, expressive faces with clear emotions, but every character has their own face shape, nose, eyes, hair, build and age exactly as described, so no two characters look alike. Base palette of moss green, spruce green, lingonberry red, birch white, fjord teal, honey yellow and warm earth browns, shifted towards each story's own season, light and colours. The pictures are the colour plates of a classic printed storybook with warm cream pages, so the lightest tones are warm and creamy, never stark white, and lingonberry red is the accent that comes back in every picture. The picture fills the whole image, with no border, frame or lettering. Costumes and places from old rural Norway: spruce and birch forests, fjords and mountains, turf-roofed log houses, carved wood, rosemaling patterns, wool and linen clothes. Kings and princesses wear simple storybook finery. Trolls are huge, lumpy and mossy and look silly rather than scary. Clear, uncluttered composition with one focal point and room to breathe. Cosy, safe and full of wonder. Not photorealistic, not 3D, not anime, no glossy digital look.
