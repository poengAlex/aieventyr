Character: the Devil. A short, pot-bellied devil no taller than a boy of ten, with shiny tomato-red skin, a round face with a button nose, big goggly amber eyes, pointy ears, a long, pointed black goatee, two big black ram's horns curling out of his forehead and a very long, thin tail with an arrow-shaped tip. He wears a long black overcoat, a soot-black waistcoat with shiny brass buttons stretched over his belly and polished black shoes far too fine for gravel. Vain and silly rather than frightening.

Story world: Present-day Norway, a farming valley in summer. The Smith is the national champion farrier. His workshop is a long, dark-red steel-clad building by a county road, with a gravel yard for horse trailers, an old pear tree heavy with yellow pears against the side wall and his old mother's small white house next door. Inside are a coal forge with an electric fan and a big anvil, and in the break room a worn armchair patched with grey gaffer tape on one armrest. The steel-wire bag is a small, round coin purse of braided silver-grey steel wire with a snap clasp. Far along the road, the way forks: one road climbs into white clouds to a tall, pale gate; the other drops into a smoky gully to a tall black steel gate with an intercom and nine locks.

This story's season, light and colours: A hot, bright Norwegian summer: dusty gravel, yellow fields and green verges, cow parsley in the ditches and ripe yellow pears in the low evening sun. Inside the workshop: charcoal greys lit by the orange forge and white sparks. At the end, the gate of Heaven glows pale gold in white cloud, and the gate of Hell stands in plum-grey smoke.

Present-day Norway: fjord towns, suburbs, farms with tractors, cabins, ferries, roadside petrol stations, offices and flats, grey rain and bright snow, with people in modern clothes such as rain jackets, knitted sweaters, hoodies, suits and work wear. The magic of the tale appears plainly in this everyday world: trolls, talking animals and enchantments look real and slightly absurd, drawn in the same flat style. Every character has their own face, build, age, hair and clothes exactly as described, so no two characters look alike.
