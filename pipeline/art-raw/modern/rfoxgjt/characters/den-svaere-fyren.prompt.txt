Story world: Present-day Norway in late August. Dama has a smallholding at the far end of a valley: a white farmhouse with a red barn, a gravel yard with a wooden gate, and a tractor track down to a river and a birch grove, with twelve goats, twenty sheep and three cows. She works full-time at the health centre. The three men who want the herdsman job are really a bear, a wolf and a fox in human shape, with only small hints: the big one's fur-like beard, the thin one's yellow eyes, and the red-bearded one's bushy tail, hidden under his jacket until the very end.
