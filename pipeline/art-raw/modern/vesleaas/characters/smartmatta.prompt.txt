Character: the Smart Mat. A round black rubber mat about the size of a bath mat, with a smooth, milky-white stone disc set into its centre, faint swirls of pale gold and rose inside the stone, and a small round speaker grille at one edge with a tiny light. When it speaks, or someone steps on it, the stone and the light glow a soft, warm gold.

Story world: Present-day Norway in spring and summer. The royal palace is a big cream-white palace in a park with clipped lawns, rose beds and tulip beds, a pond with reeds, broad stone main stairs and a tall wrought-iron gate, and wet fields lie behind it. More than two hundred white and grey geese, protected by royal decree since 1791, go wherever they like. Aase, the palace goose keeper, sits on the low stone edge by the gate and sews patches onto her one pair of work trousers. The prince from England lives in the guest wing and is driven in and out in a long black car. His Smart Mat is a round black rubber mat with an ancient truth stone set into it and a little speaker. His signet ring is a heavy gold ring with a flat seal that locks.

This story's season, light and colours: Spring into summer: fresh green lawns, pink and red tulips, white and grey geese, clear, cool morning light and one low, foggy morning over wet grass. The night scenes are a hushed dark blue, lit only by the mat's small golden glow.
