Character: the Black Grouse. A large, calm black grouse cock, a little bigger than the rooster, with glossy blue-black plumage, a bright-red fleshy arch like an eyebrow above each eye, a white bar across each wing, a fluffy snow-white puff under his tail and a lyre-shaped tail whose outer feathers curl outwards like hooks. He has feathered grey legs, a short black beak and wise dark eyes, stands tall like a judge, and always holds a phone in a green case in one wing.

Story world: Present-day rural Norway. The Rooster, the Cuckoo and the Black Grouse are ordinary-sized birds who live like neighbours around the same small farmyard: three old wooden houses, one red, one yellow and one white, around a gravel yard with a barn, a flagpole and a few round, white-wrapped silage bales. They have phones, a group chat and one cow between them, bought on a Saturday from a seller in the car park of a shopping centre: a flat grey tarmac lot with painted parking bays, trolleys and rows of cars in front of a big, windowless shopping centre. The birds and the cow wear no clothes.

This story's season, light and colours: A Saturday in May under a flat, white overcast sky over grey tarmac; then twelve minutes past four the next morning: a pale lemon-and-lilac sky before sunrise, cool blue shadows in the farmyard, dew on the gravel and the first windows lighting up. Accents: the Rooster's scarlet comb, the Cow's russet coat and her blue rope halter.

Art style: Contemporary Scandinavian editorial illustration: flat, graphic shapes with subtle risograph grain and a little hand-drawn texture, clean confident lines and a limited palette of spot inks led by deep petrol blue, with fjord teal, lingonberry red, honey mustard, sage green, warm paper off-white and ink charcoal, like the pages of a risograph-printed book, shifted towards each story's own season, light and colours, in the cool, clear Nordic light of today. Present-day Norway: fjord towns, suburbs, farms with tractors, cabins, ferries, roadside petrol stations, offices and flats, grey rain and bright snow, with people in modern clothes such as rain jackets, knitted sweaters, hoodies, suits and work wear. The magic of the tale appears plainly in this everyday world: trolls, talking animals and enchantments look real and slightly absurd, drawn in the same flat style. Every character has their own face, build, age, hair and clothes exactly as described, so no two characters look alike. Dry humour, careful composition with one focal point and plenty of negative space. The picture fills the whole image, with no border, frame or lettering. Not photorealistic, not 3D, not anime, no glossy digital look.
