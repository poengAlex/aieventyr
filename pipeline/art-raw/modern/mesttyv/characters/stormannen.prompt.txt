A stout, self-important man of about fifty-five with a huge round belly, a pink double chin, rosy jowls, small twinkling eyes, a snub nose, a neat grey moustache waxed into two curly tips and grey hair curling over his ears. He wears a tobacco-brown tweed jacket over a canary-yellow knitted waistcoat stretched tight across his belly, a pale-blue shirt, a gold watch, grey trousers and shiny brown brogues.

The farmer drives an old pickup with a livestock trailer; his three oxen are big, reddish-brown and white-faced, with short curved horns. The vicarage garden has a great old maple. The barrel is a big oak barrel on a rickety cart pulled by a bony old brown mare in a harness of willow withies and twigs.
