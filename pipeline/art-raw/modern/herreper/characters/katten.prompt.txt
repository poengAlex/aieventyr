Character: the Cat. Around her neck she wears an old, worn collar of faded red leather with a small tarnished brass buckle. She sits bolt upright, like a general inspecting her troops.

A main road with steel guard rails runs through dark spruce forest to the royal estate: a large white manor with guards, fences and cameras at the front, and at the back a delivery yard with a loading bay, rubbish containers and a gravel courtyard. Per waits in a rented bedsit in a shabby block of flats behind the railway. Herreper drives a low, dark, gleaming sports car; the King rides in a long black car with advisers, security men with earpieces and four Royal Guard riders carrying small Norwegian flags. Far away lies the Silver Estate, where everything inside is gold, even the light switches. The pot is a black cast-iron pot, the griddle a big, flat, round iron griddle.
