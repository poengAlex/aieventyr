Place: the Farmhouse Kitchen. A cosy, cluttered farmhouse kitchen with pale wood-panelled walls: a pine table with mismatched chairs, a black wood stove with a kettle on top, a fridge covered with children's drawings and magnets, rag rugs on a worn wooden floor and a steep, narrow staircase up to the children's rooms. A short hallway with coats, caps and boots on pegs leads to the white front door.

Story world: Present-day Norway, on a steep valley side in autumn. Gudbrand's smallholding sits so high up the slope that the snowplough turns in his yard: a small red wooden farmhouse with white window frames, an old grey cowshed, a little brew house with an old stable-style half-door, and a gravel yard. The neighbour's yellow house stands just below the driveway. Gudbrand drives a battered old dark-green estate car with a tow bar. Down in the valley lie the market town, a roundabout with a petrol station, bus shelters and farm shops. The animals: a black cow with a white face, a brown horse, a fat pink pig, a white milking goat, a woolly white sheep, a big white goose and a glossy cinnamon-brown rooster.

This story's season, light and colours: A cold, bright late-October day: a pale-blue sky, the last yellow birch leaves and rust-red bracken on the valley sides, wet grey tarmac, frost in the shadows and long amber afternoon light; then a clear, cold blue evening with warm yellow light in the farmhouse windows. Accents: the wife’s cornflower-blue apron, the red farmhouse and the orange of hi-vis.

Art style: Contemporary Scandinavian editorial illustration: flat, graphic shapes with subtle risograph grain and a little hand-drawn texture, clean confident lines and a limited palette of spot inks led by deep petrol blue, with fjord teal, lingonberry red, honey mustard, sage green, warm paper off-white and ink charcoal, like the pages of a risograph-printed book, shifted towards each story's own season, light and colours, in the cool, clear Nordic light of today. The magic of the tale appears plainly in this everyday world: trolls, talking animals and enchantments look real and slightly absurd, drawn in the same flat style. Dry humour, careful composition with one focal point and plenty of negative space. The picture fills the whole image, with no border, frame or lettering.
