The same small, bold boy, the size of a child of about six, with a round face, rosy cheeks, a small button nose, big bright cornflower-blue eyes, a determined little chin and a mop of golden curls under his oversized blue-and-white striped beanie. The royal estate is an old castle of dark stone at the tip of a headland, rebuilt with glass and antennas, with towers, a gate, a park, a quay and a big old-fashioned kitchen with a wood-fired range; below it lies a wide sandy beach with a big old oak. While the princess is promised to the trolls, black mourning flags hang from the towers. The trolls come over the sea in black ships. Lillekort's sword looks like scrap wrapped in old newspaper until its blade catches the light. His ship folds up as small as a matchbox of shiny metal and unfolds, panel by panel, into a sea-going ship with lights in its hull. The princess's three secret garments are thin as foil.
