Character: the Rooster. A tall, handsome rooster speckled all over in rust red and bright gold like autumn leaves, with a shining golden cape of neck feathers, a fluffy golden beard puffing out under his beak, a small red comb, hazel eyes, a yellow beak, sturdy yellow legs and a sweeping tail of long, dark bronze feathers. He has a relaxed, evasive air and likes to shrug with his wings.

Story world: Present-day Norway, on the last smallholding before a small town begins. The hen and the rooster live in a little red-painted wooden chicken coop with a cold concrete floor and doorstep, straw bedding, a wooden nest box along one wall and a wire-mesh run beside it. A coral-red garden gate in the white picket fence opens onto a street with a pavement and lamp posts, and a little way down it the town's main street begins, with shop fronts, parked bicycles and a bus stop. The hen's egg basket is a small, round wicker basket with a handle, lined with straw and holding six brown eggs. The birds wear no clothes, and the hen's feet are bare.
