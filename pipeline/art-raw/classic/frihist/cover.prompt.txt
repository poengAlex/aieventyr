Create the cover illustration for an illustrated folk-tale book, in a square format. Do not write a title or any other text on it.

Picture: On a frosty late-winter afternoon, the Suitor, in his Sunday best and tall hat, with his little travel bag in one hand, walks up a trodden path through deep snow to a tiny, lopsided grey cottage with a sagging, snow-covered turf roof and long icicles on the eaves. At its one small window, three curious faces crowd together to peer out at him: the Farmer, the Farmer's Wife and the Daughter in her new green cap. Low pink sun and lilac-blue shadows.

- the Suitor: A tall, gangly young man of about twenty with a round, eager face, apple-pink cheeks, a small blunt nose, hopeful pale-blue eyes, a nervous smile and neat butter-blond hair parted in the middle and slicked down flat, except for one unruly cowlick at the back. He wears his Sunday best: a slightly too short cinnamon-brown wool jacket with brass buttons, a white shirt with a black neckerchief, grey striped trousers, polished black boots and a tall black hat.
- the Farmer: A big, burly, barrel-chested farmer of about fifty with a broad, ruddy face, a small snub nose, twinkling little blue eyes, bushy brown eyebrows, a shock of unruly brown hair and a long brown beard that splits into two scraggly forks. - the Farmer's Wife: A small, wiry, bustling woman of about forty-five with a narrow, freckled face, a long pointed nose, bright little grey eyes, a busy, pursed mouth and frizzy ginger hair escaping from a patched brown headscarf. - the Daughter: A cheeky farm lass of eighteen with a long face, a sharp chin, a snub, upturned nose, big round brown eyes, rosy cheeks, a little rosebud mouth and thick black hair in two short, stiff plaits that poke straight out to the sides. She wears a patched, faded brown pinafore dress over a grey blouse, and on her head a brand-new, bright emerald-green knitted cap with a big white tassel, which she is very proud of.
