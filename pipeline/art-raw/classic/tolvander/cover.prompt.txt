Picture: Sunrise on the White Bog, the first low light catching every tuft of bog cotton. The Maiden stands knee-deep among the white tufts with her arms full of bog cotton and her lips pressed together in silence, and looks up as twelve wild ducks fly low over her head in a long line against the pale-gold sky. A full sack rests on a tussock beside her.

Accents of pure white, blood red and raven black.
