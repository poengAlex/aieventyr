Character: Askeladden. A tall, gangly young prince of about nineteen with long arms and legs, a long face with deep dimples when he smiles, warm hazel eyes, a straight nose and copper-red hair cut in a round bowl cut. He wears a sky-blue linen smock with a small silver clasp at the collar, belted with a broad brown leather belt, loose grey wool breeches and tall, worn brown riding boots.

Story world: Old rural Norway in early summer. The King's manor is a large royal farm of dark log buildings with turf roofs, carved dragon gables and a wide yard, among flowering meadows. The road from the manor runs through the meadows past the tussock where the Doll in the Grass lives: a round, knee-high hump of grass and clover that parts like a curtain, with a snug little room beneath it. The tiny shirt is snow-white linen, no longer than a man's finger. Her carriage is an ordinary shining silver tablespoon drawn by two little white mice with reins of red thread. Further on, the road runs along a great, wide, dark lake fringed with reeds. Askeladden's horse is a dun fjord horse with a black-and-cream striped mane.

This story's season, light and colours: Early summer: meadows full of buttercups, clover, cornflowers and white daisies, fresh green birches and soft morning light under a pale-blue sky with small white clouds. The great lake is dark slate-blue under drifting cloud. The homecoming is a grey, rain-washed afternoon with puddles and dripping eaves. Accents: shining silver, grass green and sky blue.

Art style: Classic Norwegian folk-tale book illustration: fine pen-and-ink line work with transparent watercolour washes on warm, slightly textured off-white paper, in the tradition of 19th-century Scandinavian book illustration, like the plates of an old printed book of folk tales, with a deep red that comes back as the accent. The picture fills the whole image, with no border, frame or lettering. Naturalistic proportions and expressive, characterful faces; every character has their own face, build, age and hair exactly as described, so no two characters look alike. Muted natural palette of fjord teal, slate blue, moss green, lichen grey, honey ochre, lingonberry red, ink black and birch white, shifted towards each story's own season, light and colours, with the warm cream paper showing through the washes, with dramatic Nordic light: low sun, long shadows, blue dusk and firelight. Old rural Norway: dark spruce and pine forests, steep mountains, fjords, turf-roofed log farms, carved wood, homespun wool and linen. Trolls are enormous, ancient and earthy, like pieces of the landscape come alive, made of moss, bark, stone and lichen, and more grotesque-comic than frightening. Kings and princesses wear modest medieval finery. Careful composition with depth and atmosphere, one clear focal point and room for the landscape. Not cute or cartoonish, not photorealistic, not 3D, not anime, no glossy digital look.
