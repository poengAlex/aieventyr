Character: the Girl. A slim, cheerful hunter's daughter of about eighteen with an oval face dusted all over with freckles, a snub nose, big hazel eyes, a wide smile and mousy-brown hair in one thick plait over her shoulder. She wears a plain pine-green linen dress with the sleeves rolled up, a short brown sheepskin vest, a leather belt and sturdy brown shoes, and has a blue jay feather tucked behind one ear.

Story world: Old rural Norway, long ago. The royal manor is a big cluster of dark log buildings with turf roofs, carved gables and small leaded windows around a cobbled yard. The flax is soft, pale-gold fibre in a great heap; spun, it becomes smooth, shining pale-gold thread wound in coils; woven, it becomes a thick bolt of fine cream-white linen; sewn, it becomes a pile of white linen shirts with buttons. The Queen's other maids are three young women in grey wool dresses, white aprons and white linen caps. The wedding feast is held in the Great Hall, with a long table, candles and six guests in festive clothes.

This story's season, light and colours: A long, rainy early autumn at the royal manor: silver rain on small leaded windows, dripping gardens with the first yellow leaves and soft grey-violet skies, then fresh, rain-washed mornings with sun on wet cobbles. Indoors: warm candle and firelight on pale-gold thread, cream linen and white shirts, and a wedding feast glowing with candles and red-and-green rosemaling. Accents: flax gold and the Queen's rainbow-striped knitting.

Art style: Classic Norwegian folk-tale book illustration: fine pen-and-ink line work with transparent watercolour washes on warm, slightly textured off-white paper, in the tradition of 19th-century Scandinavian book illustration, like the plates of an old printed book of folk tales, with a deep red that comes back as the accent. The picture fills the whole image, with no border, frame or lettering. Naturalistic proportions and expressive, characterful faces; every character has their own face, build, age and hair exactly as described, so no two characters look alike. Muted natural palette of fjord teal, slate blue, moss green, lichen grey, honey ochre, lingonberry red, ink black and birch white, shifted towards each story's own season, light and colours, with the warm cream paper showing through the washes, with dramatic Nordic light: low sun, long shadows, blue dusk and firelight. Old rural Norway: dark spruce and pine forests, steep mountains, fjords, turf-roofed log farms, carved wood, homespun wool and linen. Trolls are enormous, ancient and earthy, like pieces of the landscape come alive, made of moss, bark, stone and lichen, and more grotesque-comic than frightening. Kings and princesses wear modest medieval finery. Careful composition with depth and atmosphere, one clear focal point and room for the landscape. Not cute or cartoonish, not photorealistic, not 3D, not anime, no glossy digital look.
