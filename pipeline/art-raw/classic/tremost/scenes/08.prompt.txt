Create one new illustration for an illustrated folk-tale book, showing the moment described below.

Picture: Late in the wedding feast in the Great Hall, the candles burning low. The Prince has risen at the long table and makes his declaration with one hand raised. Beside him the Bride keeps her face grave and her hands folded, but her eyes are shining. Along the table the three aunts nod solemnly: the Long-Nosed Aunt tapping her nose, the Broad-Hipped Aunt patting her hips and the Teary-Eyed Aunt dabbing her eyes.

Characters in this picture (nobody else, unless the picture description mentions them):
- the Prince: A tall, slender, polite young prince of about twenty with a long, clean-shaven face, a dimpled chin, a long nose with a little bump, earnest dark eyes under thick eyebrows and short, tidy black curls. He wears a small silver circlet, a deep-teal wool tunic with a row of silver buttons, a cream linen shirt, charcoal breeches and tall tan leather boots.
- the Bride: A slim young bride of about eighteen with an oval face dusted with freckles, a snub nose, big hazel eyes and a wide smile, her mousy-brown hair brushed out long and wavy over her shoulders under a tall silver bridal crown hung with little dangling spangles. She wears a long cream wool dress embroidered with red and green flowers at the hem and cuffs, a red woven sash, and a blue jay feather tucked behind one ear.
- the Long-Nosed Aunt: A small, slim, spry old woman of about eighty who holds herself very upright, with a kindly, wrinkled face, merry little blue eyes, rosy cheeks and crooked, work-worn hands. Her nose is enormous: thick as a cucumber at the root and nearly six feet long, it sticks straight out in front of her like a branch and ends in a round pink tip. Her white hair is in a small knot. She wears a long nut-brown dress and a big moss-green fringed shawl, and leans on a crooked walking stick.
- the Broad-Hipped Aunt: A short old woman of about eighty with narrow shoulders, a small round head and enormously broad hips and backside, so wide that she can only just squeeze through a door and her skirt looks like a haystack. She has a square little face, a snub nose, bright squinting eyes, a gap-toothed grin and grey hair in a tight bun under a small round black felt hat. She wears a huge, bell-shaped skirt with wide red and ochre stripes, a white blouse and a short dark-brown knitted jacket.
- the Teary-Eyed Aunt: A round-shouldered old woman of about eighty, of middle height, with a soft, wrinkled face and enormous round eyes as big as small plates, red-rimmed and always running with tears, so she keeps dabbing them with a big white lace handkerchief. Her silver hair is braided and pinned in a ring on top of her head. She wears a dusty-rose dress with a white collar, a red tomato-shaped pincushion on her wrist and a silver thimble on one finger.
