Story world: Old rural Norway, where the farm birds can talk. The hen and the rooster live on a small hillside farm with a little weathered log chicken coop on short stilts, a plank ramp up to its tiny door, a stacked woodpile and a grey split-rail fence with a gate. From the gate a dusty cart road winds down through birches to a small market town far off in the valley, with a white church and red and yellow wooden houses. The hen's egg basket is a small, round wicker basket with a handle, lined with straw and holding six brown eggs; she carries it hooked over one wing. The birds wear no clothes, and the hen goes barefoot.
