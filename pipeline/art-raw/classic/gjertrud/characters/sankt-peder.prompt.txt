Character: St Peter. A short, sturdy, round-shouldered old man of about sixty-five with a round, cheerful face, a shiny bald head with a fringe of short grey hair around the back, big ears, a broad flat nose, bright brown eyes and a short, bristly grey beard. He wears a knee-length faded green tunic with a leather belt, brown woollen leggings, a short brown hooded cape and worn leather boots, with a small bronze key hanging from his belt. No halo.

Story world: Old rural Norway, in the days when Our Lord and St Peter still wandered the earth. Gjertrud's home is a small, low log cottage with a mossy turf roof and a squat grey stone chimney, alone at the edge of a spruce forest. Inside is one smoky, firelit room: an open stone hearth with a smoke hood and chimney above it, a big, round, flat iron griddle over the embers, a long wooden baking board dusted with flour, a rolling pin and a wooden bench by the door. The flatbreads are lefser: big, thin and round, pale with brown speckles. Every one Gjertrud rolls, however tiny the scrap of dough, spreads on the griddle until it covers it from rim to rim.

This story's season, light and colours: A raw, wet afternoon in early spring: grey snow melting in the shadows, dark dripping spruce, bare birches with swelling buds, brown puddles on the path and heavy slate-grey rain clouds rolling in. Inside the cottage: warm amber firelight on smoky timber, golden-brown flatbread and pale flour. Accents: the bright scarlet of the red cap and the deep black of soot.

Art style: Classic Norwegian folk-tale book illustration: fine pen-and-ink line work with transparent watercolour washes on warm, slightly textured off-white paper, in the tradition of 19th-century Scandinavian book illustration, like the plates of an old printed book of folk tales, with a deep red that comes back as the accent. The picture fills the whole image, with no border, frame or lettering. Naturalistic proportions and expressive, characterful faces; every character has their own face, build, age and hair exactly as described, so no two characters look alike. Muted natural palette of fjord teal, slate blue, moss green, lichen grey, honey ochre, lingonberry red, ink black and birch white, shifted towards each story's own season, light and colours, with the warm cream paper showing through the washes, with dramatic Nordic light: low sun, long shadows, blue dusk and firelight. Old rural Norway: dark spruce and pine forests, steep mountains, fjords, turf-roofed log farms, carved wood, homespun wool and linen. Trolls are enormous, ancient and earthy, like pieces of the landscape come alive, made of moss, bark, stone and lichen, and more grotesque-comic than frightening. Kings and princesses wear modest medieval finery. Careful composition with depth and atmosphere, one clear focal point and room for the landscape. Not cute or cartoonish, not photorealistic, not 3D, not anime, no glossy digital look.
