Picture: The Frozen Lake on a bitter, frosty morning. Far out on the snow-dusted ice, the Bear kneels beside a small round hole with his long bushy tail dangling down into the black water, his fur white with hoarfrost and his eyes squeezed shut against the cold. A good way off, on a snowy boulder by the shore, the Fox sits and watches with a sly grin, his bundle of five silver fish beside him.

Characters in this picture (nobody else, unless the picture description mentions them):
- the Bear: A huge, heavy, slow-moving brown bear, taller than a man when he rears up, with thick, shaggy dark chocolate-brown winter fur, a broad head with small round ears, small, gentle black eyes under heavy brows, a short greyish-tan muzzle with a big black nose and a patient, slightly puzzled look. - the Fox: A slim, light-footed, long-legged red fox with sleek, bright flame-red winter fur, a snow-white chin, throat and chest, black lower legs and paws, tall pointed ears with black backs, narrow amber eyes with a sly sideways glance and a long, pointed snout with long whiskers. His big, bushy red tail ends in a clean white tip.

Setting:
- the Frozen Lake: A small, round forest lake frozen solid, its thick ice pale grey-blue and dusted with snow, ringed by tall, snow-laden spruces and a few bare birches. Snowy reeds poke up along the shore, round boulders wear caps of snow, and the dark edge of the spruce forest comes right down to the shore.

Story world: A snowy spruce forest in old Norway, long ago, where the animals can talk. Below the forest lies the Frozen Lake, a small forest lake under thick, snow-dusted ice. A little way up the hill is a sunny slope, an open clearing where the low winter sun shines, with one dense, dark spruce bush at its upper edge. The fox's stolen fish are five fat silver fish tied together by their tails with a piece of string. The bear and the fox are natural wild animals without clothes: they walk on four legs, but can sit up and use their front paws like hands.

This story's season, light and colours: Deep midwinter in a spruce forest: a bitter, frosty morning of hoarfrost and pale blue light over the frozen lake, then, just before Christmas, a still, clear day when the low sun lies warm and golden on a snowy slope, with long blue shadows towards evening. Accents: the fox's flame red, butter yellow and dark spruce green.
