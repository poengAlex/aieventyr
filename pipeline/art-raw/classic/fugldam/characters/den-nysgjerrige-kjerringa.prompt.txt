Character: the Nosy Old Woman. A short, bustling old woman with a plump little body on thin legs, like a robin, and a long neck that she cranes forward to see everything. She has a sharp little chin, a beaky nose, big curious eyes magnified by thick, round spectacles and wispy white hair scraped into a tiny topknot like an onion. She wears a faded lavender dress, an orange knitted shawl and a patched brown apron with a deep pocket.

Story world: Old Norway by the sea, over fourteen years of voyages. The princesses' father rules a royal manor of big log halls above a sandy beach. The twelve princes sail in one big, round-bellied wooden ship. On a small rocky island stands the troll prince's castle, with a tall hall of pale stone columns; far across the sea, the Black Castle on a rocky shore holds the Troll King's gilded chamber. The little dog on the island is fluffy and white, with floppy brown ears. The old sword is long and rusty, with a plain iron hilt; the troll's bottle is round, of dark-green glass. The crown cupboard is waist-high, painted blue with red-and-white rosemaling, and holds twelve small golden crowns. The iron boat is small and dark, with no oars or sail. The eleven older princes wear matching grey-blue coats; the eleven older princesses wear long dresses in soft colours.

This story's season, light and colours: The open northern sea in all its moods, from howling gales to glassy calm: jade-green and pewter-grey waves with white foam, towering silver-grey clouds, pale luminous summer nights and clear rosy dawns. Indoors, warm lamplight and the rich glow of gold. Accents: Sir Red's scarlet, the prince's forest green and gleaming golden crowns.

Art style: Classic Norwegian folk-tale book illustration: fine pen-and-ink line work with transparent watercolour washes on warm, slightly textured off-white paper, in the tradition of 19th-century Scandinavian book illustration, like the plates of an old printed book of folk tales, with a deep red that comes back as the accent. The picture fills the whole image, with no border, frame or lettering. Naturalistic proportions and expressive, characterful faces; every character has their own face, build, age and hair exactly as described, so no two characters look alike. Muted natural palette of fjord teal, slate blue, moss green, lichen grey, honey ochre, lingonberry red, ink black and birch white, shifted towards each story's own season, light and colours, with the warm cream paper showing through the washes, with dramatic Nordic light: low sun, long shadows, blue dusk and firelight. Old rural Norway: dark spruce and pine forests, steep mountains, fjords, turf-roofed log farms, carved wood, homespun wool and linen. Trolls are enormous, ancient and earthy, like pieces of the landscape come alive, made of moss, bark, stone and lichen, and more grotesque-comic than frightening. Kings and princesses wear modest medieval finery. Careful composition with depth and atmosphere, one clear focal point and room for the landscape. Not cute or cartoonish, not photorealistic, not 3D, not anime, no glossy digital look.
